# Free movies, legally

Availability depends on your country and changes often. Check each service in your region.

- **YouTube:** many films are free with ads. Use the Movies section of the YouTube app.
- **Tubi:** free, ad-supported movies and series (package `com.tubitv` in `catalog/apps.json`).
- **Pluto TV:** free live channels and on-demand films, ad-supported.
- **Plex:** free channels, plus your own media library if you have one.
- **Amazon Prime Video:** some films are free with ads; the rest need Prime.
- **Internet Archive (archive.org):** public-domain films, in a browser or with an app.
- **Library cards:** some libraries include Kanopy or Hoopla with free films. Check your library's website.

The skill installs the apps you pick and never installs apps from sources it does not list.
